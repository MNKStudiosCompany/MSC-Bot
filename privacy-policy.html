Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 21, 2026

This policy describes how MNK Studios (MSC) may handle information when you visit our website or interact with our services. Review and update it to reflect the technologies and services actually used on your website.

01. Introduction

MNK Studios (MSC) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, why it may be used, and what choices may be available to you.

02. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use our services, information may include:

  • Information you provide: messages, support requests, or information you voluntarily submit.
  • Technical information: browser type, device information, basic diagnostic data and server logs, where available to our hosting provider.
  • Usage information: pages visited and general interaction data if analytics tools are enabled.
  • Third-party information: information processed by platforms you choose to use, subject to their own policies.

We should only collect information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

03. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to:

  • Operate, maintain and secure our website.
  • Respond to support requests and questions.
  • Identify technical issues and improve our services.
  • Prevent spam, abuse, fraud and unauthorized access.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not claim to collect or use information for purposes that are not actually part of our operations.

04. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies if required by hosting, security, analytics or advertising services.

Cookies may help services function, remember settings, measure usage or support advertising. The specific cookies depend on which third-party services are enabled.

You may be able to manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect some website features.

05. Advertising

If advertising services are enabled on an MSC website or game, advertising providers may process information such as cookie identifiers, device information, IP addresses or advertising interaction data, as permitted by their policies and applicable law.

Some providers may use this information to deliver, measure or personalize advertisements. The information collected and available choices depend on the provider and the advertising configuration actually used.

You can learn more about Google's advertising practices at Google's Advertising Policy ↗ .

06. Third-Party Services

MSC may use or link to third-party services such as:

  • GitHub Pages or other website hosting providers.
  • Discord for community communication.
  • Google services, if integrated into our projects.
  • Advertising or analytics providers, if enabled.

These services may process information under their own privacy policies. Review their policies before providing personal information directly to them.

07. Sharing of Information

MSC does not sell your personal information as a business practice. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate our services, or disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect security and rights.

Any sharing should be limited to what is necessary for the relevant purpose and handled in accordance with applicable law.

08. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information from unauthorized access, loss or misuse. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for its purpose, subject to legal and security needs. Actual retention periods depend on the systems and providers used.

09. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, or to object to certain processing.

You can also manage browser cookies and use available controls offered by third-party platforms. Contact MSC to submit a privacy-related request.

10. Children's Privacy

MSC does not intend to collect personal information from children in ways prohibited by applicable law. Where consent or additional safeguards are legally required, those requirements should be met.

If you believe personal information has been submitted improperly, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

11. External Links

Our website may link to external websites. MSC does not control their content, security or privacy practices. Their own policies apply when you visit those websites.

12. International Processing

Third-party service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Any such processing is subject to the provider's practices and applicable legal requirements.

13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when our services, technologies or legal obligations change. The latest version and update date will be published on this page.

14. Contact MSC

For privacy questions or requests, contact our team through the official MSC Discord server. Avoid posting sensitive personal information in public channels.
